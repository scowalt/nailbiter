# Pregame hype evidence

Hype describes pregame watch-worthiness through independent Stakes, Quality and Closeness components. An unavailable component remains unavailable; no missing input is silently replaced with a neutral score. The shared scorer in `apps/backend.shared/Scoring/ProHypePolicy.cs` and the real importer/page tests are the executable contract.

## Eligibility and source clocks

Require verified sport/season/team/start identity, eligible unstarted game state, consistent competition context and fresh evidence. MLB alone permits a scheduled scoreless first inning. A successful invalid observation revokes eligibility; transport failure preserves previous values and their original clocks. Fetching old publication content does not renew its publication age.

## Last-known-good display

For NFL/NBA/NHL/MLB, `IProHypeLifecycle` separates strict current calculation from a complete saved preview. Permitted age, transport and completed-result coverage gaps neither expire an applicable whole snapshot nor renew its original observations. New complete valid evidence replaces it; changed identity/schedule/phase/status, rejected supporting evidence or revoked source selection prevents reuse. Pregame stops at scheduled kickoff or confirmed play, including canonical `in_progress`, whichever comes first. Without a valid saved snapshot, existing unavailable/partial-component behavior remains unchanged.

Home, league and detail consumers resolve the same whole result and authoritative game identity. Current ordinary odds and team information remain independent. Required derived-score credit follows the evidence that actually produced the saved estimate, not a newer selected source. No stale badge, timestamp, hidden diagnostic payload or new public endpoint is introduced. See the [canonical specification](https://github.com/scowalt/nailbiter/issues/17) and [offline verification](testing/pro-hype-continuity.md).

## Start hype on live games

Each pregame resolution of an upcoming, unstarted game also writes its displayed total and derived-score credit to `start_hype`, or deletes the row when hype is unavailable. College football games starting within a day are written by the same 30-second background loop. The cutoff that ends pregame also ends these writes, so the row holds the score shown at the start. The home page shows it beside excitement on live cards and orders **Live now** by the mean of start hype and excitement, using whichever is available and placing games with neither last in start order. A game in which either team's current win probability is above 99% ranks after every other live game, because its result is all but settled. Its card's probability bar shows that team at `>99%` or `100%`. These decided games keep the same ordering among themselves. League and detail pages do not show it. `039_StartHype.sql` adds the table.

## Durable lifecycle

The proposed additive `035_ProHypeLifecycle.sql` installs snapshots and evidence-writer journal/fence triggers after the existing series migration 034. It does not populate scores, rewrite input rows or backfill history. Isolated fixtures replay both migrations; `EnsureCreated` alone does not install the journal. Production application remains separately gated.

Pro-hype locks follow one order. A transaction takes the fenced table lock first, then the `pro_hype_revision` row, then any `games` rows. The fence triggers impose this order on each `INSERT`, `UPDATE` or `DELETE` of a fenced table. Capture and any code that locks a `games` row before a fenced write call `ExcitementDbContext.LockProHypeFenceAsync` first. Capture passes `ReadEvidence`, which takes `ACCESS SHARE` on `games`, `team_ratings` and `pregame_odds`. Code that locks a `games` row passes `WriteGames`, which takes `ROW EXCLUSIVE` on `games`. A different order can deadlock (40P01) with a fenced write or a migration replay. The deadlock tests in `ProHypeCaptureTests`, `PlayoffSeriesLockOrderTests` and `HighlightsApplicationTests` fail if a caller changes the order.

Registered background capture runs at startup and every 30 seconds against already committed evidence in the existing exclusive seven-day horizon. It makes no provider requests, owns no collection budget and does not change source flags or denial handling. Current numeric calculations still apply all original eligibility rules. Complete snapshots preserve their original component tuple, source provenance and evaluation time across restarts and concurrent work. The current regular-season formula uses saved `FormulaVersion = 5` (NFL `6`). Legacy/unversioned or incompatible regular-season tuples are not reused: a replacement needs complete current evidence under the new formula, otherwise hype remains unavailable. This does not renew source clocks, reinterpret old tuples or backfill historical scores. The transition consumes pending source/game journal changes before discarding a valid old-formula tuple and honors legacy raw-competition keys/signatures as well as current revocations; formula changes do not clear invalidation history. Legacy preseason/postseason/play-in tuples keep their unchanged formula and retention; durable source/game revocations and start cutoffs still apply. Unused seeding annotations are excluded from regular-season scoring fingerprints, so changing only that metadata cannot revoke a supported preview. NFL regular-season snapshots record each contributing conditional forecast separately from the ordinary unconditional playoff outlook; trusted final outcomes remain qualification evidence. Expired conditional observations permit retention without new raw eligibility, while rejected or overwritten supporting scenarios revoke durably. A withdrawn noncontributing outlook does not invalidate conditional Stakes.

## Verified progress versus future schedule coverage

For NBA regular seasons, ESPN may list only 80 or 81 of the 82 matchups. A fresh, season/team-verified schedule can still prove completed progress when its explicit season-labelled win/loss record exactly matches all parsed final results. Missing or contradictory records, omitted/reversed results, cancellation, duplicate events, invalid scores and other incomplete lengths remain rejected. The denominator stays 82; no fixture or matchup is invented. Other leagues keep their complete-schedule requirements.

Progress verification does not establish unpublished matchups. Existing [NHL constructive](nhl-seeding-evidence.md) and [NBA bounded](nba-seeding-evidence.md) seeding certificates remain compatibility metadata, not inputs to the qualification-only formula. Missing certificates, Cup-related proof exclusions and seeding annotations neither supply a bonus nor block regular-season Stakes. Qualification, verified progress, game identity, result coverage and source clocks remain independently required; no unpublished matchups, tiebreakers or observed results are invented.

ESPN result scores accept equivalent nonnegative integral representations such as `21`, `21.0` and object-wrapped values. Fractional, nonfinite, negative or overflowing scores never become rounded results or default zeroes. The same reader validates confirmed results, scoreless pregame state and NFL conditional-result corroboration.

## Components

- **Quality:** current-season complete same-source ranks, converted to relative strengths and combined harmonically. NHL prefers MoneyPuck rolling ranks, then the owner-selected [Power Rankings Guru single-page fallback](nhl-guru-rankings.md). Never mix providers or batches, or use forecast probabilities as ranks. MoneyPuck rank comes from row order with its existing seven-day publication policy; Guru uses explicit consecutive ranks and both fetch/publication ages strictly under 48 hours. Source clocks and supporting-source credit survive retained whole tuples.
- **Closeness:** `100 * (1 - abs(2 * homeProbability - 1))` for an eligible complementary whole pair. Preserve game-winner source preference, executable quote/depth validation and fixed expiry; do not mix qualification probabilities into matchup closeness. The composite counts `Closeness × Quality / 100`, so an even game counts only as far as the teams are good ([ADR 0006](adr/0006-quality-scaled-pro-closeness.md)). The detail page shows the unscaled Closeness and states the share that counts.
- **Stakes:** phase-specific competition consequences. On 2026-11-05 the owner removed seeding consequences from NFL/NBA/NHL/MLB regular-season scoring because there is no dedicated integrated seeding-consequence feed and local proof coverage was incomplete. Regular-season Stakes now uses qualification only, with no seeding term or availability gate. The NFL exception, from 2026-11-09, adds PlayoffStatus division-title and No. 1 seed swings ([ADR 0007](adr/0007-nfl-seeding-stakes.md)); missing seed cells leave it qualification-only. Postseason rounds, series progression and play-in variants still use verified context, not generic playoff flags or name guesses.

NFL regular-season qualification Stakes uses the verified next-game win/loss playoff swing from [conditional leverage](nfl-playoff-leverage.md), scaled by 80 without multiplying game-specific leverage by urgency again. Game Stakes is the larger team's [playoff position swing](nfl-playoff-leverage.md#scoring-and-presentation), capped at 100. Threshold forecasts keep their full lower/upper bounds: missing scenarios or unequal game-level bounds remain unavailable. Kalshi's unconditional outlook cannot replace a conditional forecast; trusted final facts stay separate.

NBA/NHL/MLB regular-season qualification uses the same model with an [estimated playoff swing](adr/0004-estimated-playoff-swing-stakes.md), because no approved source publishes their win/loss forecasts. With `remaining = scheduled - completed` (including this game), each nonfinal team contributes `80 * φ(Φ⁻¹(probability)) / sqrt(0.55² * (remaining - 1) + 0.7²)`; game Stakes is the maximum of those contributions. One game therefore matters less with more games left, and an 82- or 162-game season no longer receives NFL-sized Stakes from the fraction of the season played. The estimate is a scoring heuristic calibrated on synthetic seasons by `scripts/stakes-swing/calibrate.py`; it is never displayed as a win/loss playoff forecast. The NFL never falls back to it. Trusted Kalshi qualification finals, whether qualified or eliminated, contribute zero without unrelated progress or seeding evidence; an NFL qualified team still contributes its forecast's seeding term. Missing/invalid qualification is still unavailable, not a default zero. A confirmed qualification outcome is not a market price; model endpoints remain nonfinal estimates.

Composite weights differ by sport in the shared policy. NHL uses Stakes 0.425, Quality 0.425 and Closeness 0.15, because NHL favourites rarely produce lopsided lines and the earlier 0.30 Closeness weight lifted most games into the 60s. Other sports keep their weights. Every sport and phase applies the quality-scaled Closeness above. Beyond the explicit removal of seeding, the estimated swing, this NHL reweighting, quality-scaled Closeness and NFL seeding, there is no further weight retuning or claim of predictive accuracy. Regular-season snapshots are `FormulaVersion = 5` (NFL is `6` after NFL seeding); earlier versions are not reused.

## Synthetic coverage

Generated schedule/quote/round inputs exercise full season lengths, contradiction rejection, source freshness, first-inning eligibility, series outcomes, alias isolation, seeding-independent qualification terms, exact NFL qualification intervals and actual importer-to-page propagation. Independent short synthetic policy cases remain separate. Files are constructed, not recordings of the game IDs used as compatibility identifiers. See [fixture provenance](../tests/fixtures/README.md). NFL conditional-leverage coverage uses an independently constructed 272-contest round robin and hand-authored HTML contract fixtures, not captured pages or a schedule transcribed from them; a separate rational oracle checks the exact synthetic leverage contributions.

Public fixture replacements preserve implementation controls, not the original empirical claims. Historical captures and old evidence documents stay private and are not build/test requirements. New source collection or production data repair needs separate approval.
