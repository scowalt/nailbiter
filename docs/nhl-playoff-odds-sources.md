# NHL qualification source policy

Eligible Kalshi make/miss market evidence is preferred per team. The owner-approved MoneyPuck exception permits a separately identified model-derived fallback only under its explicit supported-season/activation controls; rankings remain an independent quality input. Model probabilities, including endpoints, are not confirmed qualification outcomes. See [ADR 0003](adr/0003-nhl-moneypuck-qualification-exception.md) and [the collector runbook](nhl-moneypuck-qualification.md).

The historical source-comparison captures and their numerical tables are retained privately, not published as a dataset or required by public development. All maintained fixture responses are independently synthetic. They test implemented selection/expiry/identity rules, not actual source availability or continuing league coverage.

Polymarket, legacy PlayoffStatus, unrelated models and paid feeds remain excluded under the existing decisions. No new scraping, permission outreach, key request, access bypass or provider activation follows from publishing this code. Publicly readable pages or SDK licenses do not establish a data license.
