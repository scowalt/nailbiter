# Constructive NHL seeding evidence

Since the owner's 2026-11-05 decision, seeding consequences are no longer part of regular-season Stakes. The proofs below describe retained compatibility metadata, not a scoring dependency or bonus. `ProCompetitionEvidenceService.ApplySeedingCertificates` still validates its fixed-position, strict record-bound and NHL constructive witnesses; provider selection, source clocks and collection budgets remain independent.

## What the witness proves

Construct two legal complete-season regulation-result assignments that differ **only in the identified game's winner**. In both assignments the two teams finish strictly above every other team, and swapping this game's result exchanges their strict first/second record order. Both therefore qualify; the exchange changes positioning or home advantage without guessing a tiebreaker. These hypothetical results are a mathematical witness of a possible consequence, not predicted outcomes or new observed results. Only the established seeding boolean/reason is persisted with the competition evidence.

The original proof required both teams to win every other non-head-to-head game. That can miss a valid exchange when a different completion includes a future loss. An independently generated 32-team, 84-game-per-team case demonstrates the gap: valid completions finish **166/164** versus **164/166**, with every other team at most **158**, but the old proof returned unknown.

## Validation and bounded search

Keep the shared full-league gates: supported season/team count and length, complete schedules, exactly two consistent copies per contest and consistent completed results. The new path additionally requires:

- Matching canonical participant ownership and home/away roles in both copies, including the target game.
- Recomputed completed IDs/counts and NHL points matching every parsed schedule, including overtime/shootout loss points.
- Every unfinished contest to be explicitly scheduled, scoreless, period zero and later than the competition observation. Live, postponed, suspended, ambiguous or stale scheduled observations do not enter this new proof path.

The deterministic search tries home-winner and cyclic-order completions for games between other teams and each possible allocation of the remaining head-to-head wins. Both target teams initially win their other unfinished games. Where needed, the higher-record team instead loses distinct legal future contests, choosing opponents with the lowest constructed totals. Completed results never change. The certificate is issued only after checking the two strict top-two completions; every hypothetical contest has one winner and awards two regulation points.

The search is sufficient, not exhaustive. Failure leaves seeding unknown. Existing proof paths are unchanged, including other sports. No outcome is inferred merely because the season is early, the teams are strong, or their playoff odds are high. Missing/invalid qualification evidence still prevents exact Stakes even if seeding is verified.

## Scoring, continuity and verification

The current formula uses qualification only, with the estimated playoff swing, unchanged sport weights and exact unrounded tuple requirements. Seeding metadata cannot change Stakes or prevent an otherwise supported score. Registered database-only capture can save a newly complete result; age/coverage gaps, hard invalidation, source credit and exclusive start cutoffs retain their existing lifecycle semantics. No historical score is reconstructed.

`NhlSeedingWitnessTests` covers the missed witness, early completed results, overtime points, missing/conflicting evidence, live/ambiguous contests, ties, inability to qualify, order invariance and Utah aliases. `NhlSeedingWitnessPageTests` runs intercepted registered competition/qualification/ranking collection through disposable PostgreSQL, automatic capture, raw scoring and home/league/detail. With deliberately high synthetic qualification probabilities, the score uses their qualification contribution regardless of the seeding proof; withdrawing executable qualification liquidity still leaves hype unavailable. It also checks original clocks, MoneyPuck admission preservation, retained Guru credit/restart and the start cutoff.

The contest controls are independently generated by `scripts/test-fixtures/generate.py`, not provider/production captures. The current qualification-only page oracle uses synthetic qualification probability **0.9**: Stakes **2.775007774069287** (84 games remaining), Quality **51625/752**, Closeness **100**, total **54.99884580603063**. The previous seeding-inclusive total is historical, not an accepted saved tuple under the new formula. Synthetic schedules exercise standings constraints, not a historically accurate calendar or live provider conformance. Actual coverage gains require normal approved collection and a reviewed rollout; tests do not authorize provider requests or production repair.
