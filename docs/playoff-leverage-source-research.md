# Conditional playoff evidence: scoped decision and remaining gates

The earlier investigation sought published qualification probabilities conditioned on the result of a specific upcoming game. Unconditional playoff probability plus game-winner probability does not identify that joint distribution. A simulator library, interactive browser simulation, screenshot or copied weekly table is not an approved recurring feed.

The owner subsequently selected **NFL-only PlayoffStatus conditional forecasts** for a separate implementation proposal. [The runbook](nfl-playoff-leverage.md) records its exact identity, result-corroboration, UTC HTTP-clock, bounded-probability and collection contracts. This source-selection decision does not establish a publisher reuse license, sustained coverage, current schema or an operational rollout.

## Preserved findings without captured data

- [PlayoffStatus NFL](https://www.playoffstatus.com/nfl/nflschedule.html) was identified as a technical next-game win/loss source. Its visible publication label alone cannot establish a UTC publication instant or current freshness. Public readability and robots guidance are not a reuse grant.
- An alternative Innerpulse source was denied to the ordinary development-host request. No alternate client, proxy, internal endpoint or retry is authorized by migration. Research-tool readability is not backend-access evidence.
- [MoneyPuck methodology](https://moneypuck.com/about.htm) discusses outcome-conditioned simulations, but Nailbiter's NHL qualification importer deliberately accepts unconditional `scenerio=ALL`. Existing approval does not validate conditional row semantics, extend source selection, replenish the provider-wide daily budget or resolve denial/activation gates.
- [nflseedR](https://github.com/nflverse/nflseedR) is simulation tooling requiring a supplied model, not an identified hosted conditional-forecast feed. Interactive scenario tools likewise do not establish a maintained downloadable publication with suitable rights/freshness.

No historical probabilities, real matchup rows, payloads, capture clocks, source-byte hashes or screenshots are copied into the canonical repository. Old research remains private inert evidence, not a required development or test dependency. The public suite uses independent generators and explicitly does not claim current provider conformance.

The [NHL PlayoffStatus rejection](adr/0002-nhl-market-only-qualification.md), [MoneyPuck-specific exception](adr/0003-nhl-moneypuck-qualification-exception.md), free-only boundary and no-bypass rules remain unchanged. Provider requests, accounts, permission outreach, paid evaluations, integration activation and production operations require their own approved scope; migrating this draft performs none of them.
