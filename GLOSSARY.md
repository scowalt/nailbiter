# Nailbiter

Nailbiter helps viewers choose sports events to watch. It describes games through excitement, team strength, and playoff stakes.

## Language

**Preseason game**:
A warm-up game outside a league's regular-season, play-in, or playoff competition, including NBA Summer League and MLB spring training. All-Star games and NBA Cup games are not preseason games.

**Pregame**:
The time before an individual game's play begins, regardless of whether it is a preseason, regular-season, or postseason game.
_Avoid_: Preseason (when referring to a game's not-yet-started state)

**Unclassified game**:
A game whose competition phase has not been established. It is not automatically a preseason or regular-season game.

**Nailbiter mark**:
The sport-independent symbol that identifies Nailbiter, distinct from team and league logos.

**Team palette**:
A team's primary and secondary brand colors, independent of its opponent.

**Matchup display color**:
The color representing a team in a particular game's probability displays. It may differ from the team's palette to distinguish the opponents, but represents that team consistently throughout the game.
_Avoid_: Team color (when referring specifically to a matchup display color)

**College football game**:
A college football game involving at least one Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) team. This includes games against Football Championship Subdivision (FCS) opponents, but not FCS-only games.

**CFP qualification**:
A team's entry into the College Football Playoff (CFP), the competition that determines the FBS national champion. This is distinct from bowl eligibility, entry into a conference championship, or winning the national championship.

**Conference championship game**:
A game that determines a conference's champion. It is distinct from a CFP game, even when its result affects CFP qualification.

**Non-CFP bowl**:
A college football bowl game outside the College Football Playoff. A bowl name alone does not establish this distinction because some bowls host CFP games.

**Playoff probability**:
The estimated chance that a team qualifies for its season's playoffs; for college football, this means CFP qualification. NBA play-in entry alone does not count, but an MLB Wild Card berth does.
_Avoid_: Championship probability, Super Bowl odds

**NFL season**:
The season identified by the year when its regular season starts. Its playoffs take place in the following calendar year.

**NBA or NHL season**:
The season identified by the year when its playoffs take place. Its regular season starts in the preceding calendar year.

**MLB season**:
The season identified by the calendar year when its regular season and postseason take place.

**Season progress**:
How far a team has advanced through its regular season, measured by completed games relative to that season's scheduled length. A verified record of zero completed games is known progress, not missing evidence.

**Completed game**:
A game whose play has ended with a final result. This describes completion, not whether the game decides a championship or a betting market has settled.

**Game highlights**:
A video recap of key plays from one completed game. It is distinct from a full replay, an isolated-play clip, or commentary alone.

**Selected game highlights**:
The single approved recap chosen for a completed game, whether automatically or by an operator. Selection does not guarantee continued playability.

**Approved highlights publisher**:
A league, team, or broadcaster accepted by Nailbiter as a source of game highlights.

**Unavailable game highlights**:
The absence of an approved, playable recap for a completed game in Nailbiter. This does not mean that the game was not completed or that no highlights exist elsewhere.

**Highlights suppression**:
A deliberate decision to stop automatic highlights selection for a particular game. It is distinct from missing highlights and does not itself prohibit an operator from choosing a recap.

**Postponed game**:
A game whose start is deferred before play begins. A postponement is not a completed game or a cancellation.

**Makeup game**:
A postponed game scheduled to be played at a later start. It is the same sporting contest, not an additional game between the teams.

**Suspended game**:
A game in which play began but was stopped for later continuation with its existing score and progress.

**Canceled game**:
A game that will not be played, rather than one awaiting a new start time.

**Championship probability**:
The estimated chance that a team wins its season's championship, not merely qualifies for the playoffs.
_Avoid_: Playoff probability

**Market-implied probability**:
An estimate of an outcome's chance inferred from gambling-market prices, rather than taken directly from a predictive model. It is not a guaranteed outcome.

**Model-derived playoff probability**:
A team's estimated chance of qualifying for its season's playoffs calculated by a predictive sports model, rather than inferred from gambling-market prices. It is distinct from a confirmed qualification outcome.

**Make/miss market**:
A gambling market on the complementary outcomes that a team qualifies or does not qualify for its season's playoffs. It is distinct from markets on winning a championship, a division, or reaching a season points total.

**Bookmaker margin**:
The amount by which a bookmaker's summed implied probabilities for a complete set of mutually exclusive outcomes exceed 100%. It is a pricing margin, not a known adjustment to any one outcome's true chance.

**Near-certain outcome bound**:
A game-outcome indication shown as `>99%` for one outcome and `<1%` for each alternative, supported by an extreme one-sided market or a confirmed outcome resolution. It is not an exact observed probability, and does not establish the score provider's game status.

**Applicable game-winner market**:
A market whose contract rules still resolve on the winner of the identified game under its current schedule. A contract that instead resolves to a cancellation or postponement fair price is not an estimate of that game's winner.

**Assumed low CFP probability**:
An assumed CFP qualification chance below 1% for an FBS team confirmed to have no qualification market for the relevant season. It is not a market-implied probability, a claim of elimination, or a substitute for an existing market with unusable quotes.

**Unavailable playoff probability**:
A playoff probability without usable evidence or an applicable explicit assumption for the relevant season. Unavailable does not mean zero or elimination.

**Settled market**:
A market with a confirmed final outcome. A settled playoff qualification market with a YES or NO outcome identifies whether the team qualified.

**Final qualification outcome**:
The confirmed fact that a team qualified or did not qualify for a particular season's playoffs. It is not an estimate of what was known before a game.

**Clinched playoffs**:
A team holds a guaranteed place in its season's playoffs, even if regular-season games remain. This does not guarantee a particular seed or a championship.

**Missed playoffs**:
A team cannot qualify for its season's playoffs, even if regular-season games remain. This does not describe a team that qualified and later lost in the playoffs.

**Settled postseason positioning**:
A qualified team's seed and relevant home-field, home-court, or home-ice advantage can no longer change through remaining regular-season results. Clinching qualification or an individual seed alone does not establish this.

**Elimination game**:
A game in which a loss ends at least one team's path to that season's championship. It does not necessarily put both teams at risk of elimination.

**Winner-take-all game**:
A game in which the winner advances or wins the championship and the loser is eliminated. A championship-deciding winner-take-all game is distinct from a game where only one team can clinch the title.

**Stakes**:
The competitive consequences of a game's result for playoff qualification or championship advancement. Stakes are distinct from team strength or the appeal of a marquee matchup.

**Playoff swing**:
The difference between a team's playoff probability if it wins a particular game and if it loses that game. It describes one game's consequence, not the team's overall playoff probability.

**Estimated playoff swing**:
Nailbiter's own estimate of a playoff swing from a team's playoff probability and games remaining, used where no published win/loss forecast exists. It is not a forecast of the team's playoff probability after a win or a loss.

**Matchup quality**:
The strength of the teams in a matchup, distinct from the game's competitive consequences or expected closeness. A matchup between strong teams can have high quality even early in the season when its stakes are low.

**Expected closeness**:
How evenly matched a game is expected to be, based on its pregame game-winner probabilities or near-certain outcome bounds. It is distinct from the game's stakes and the teams' playoff qualification probabilities.

**Hype score**:
A pregame estimate of a game's watch-worthiness based on its stakes, team strength, and expected closeness. It is distinct from excitement observed during live play.

**Probability movement**:
Changes in the estimated chance of a game outcome during play. Movement is distinct from outcome suspense: probabilities can change while the same team remains strongly favored.
_Avoid_: Variance (when referring to accumulated probability changes)

**Outcome suspense**:
Uncertainty about which team will win during play. A game can remain suspenseful without its win probabilities changing.

**Meaningful probability swing**:
A change in game-outcome prospects that rises above minor back-and-forth probability fluctuations. A swing can develop gradually rather than appearing in one observation.

**Competitive drama**:
Changes in a game's outcome prospects that matter because the result is uncertain. It is distinct from quality of play, scoring volume, and an individual viewer's enjoyment.

**Excitement score**:
A game-so-far rating of competitive drama, reflecting both meaningful probability movement and outcome suspense. It describes the whole game once completed, not its entertainment quality or excitement at a particular instant.

**Last-known-good hype score**:
The most recent complete, valid hype estimate and its original Stakes, Quality, and Closeness breakdown that still applies to the same upcoming game. Elapsed input freshness or a collection gap does not make that estimate inapplicable.

**Start hype**:
The hype score a game displayed when its pregame ended, kept unchanged while the game is live. It is not recalculated from evidence that arrives after the start.

**Unavailable hype score**:
The absence of both a complete current hype estimate and an applicable last-known-good estimate. Unavailable is distinct from a legitimate zero, and individual metrics may still be available.

**Power ranking**:
A team's relative strength compared with other teams in its league. A power ranking does not measure playoff qualification probability.

**Football Power Index (FPI)**:
ESPN's numerical estimate of a football team's strength, expressed as its expected point margin against an average opponent on a neutral field. FPI is not a poll position or a playoff seed.

**AP Top 25 ranking**:
A team's position in the Associated Press's college football poll. An unranked team is outside the poll's Top 25, not necessarily a team with unknown strength.
